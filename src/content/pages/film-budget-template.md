  - type: feature

    heading: How to use it
    text: |
      1. **Fill in quantity, unit and rate** for the lines you need. The line total and the area totals follow.
      2. **Delete what does not apply.** A documentary rarely needs construction; a commercial rarely needs a completion bond. Remove the lines, keep the area, and the formulas keep working.
      3. **Add lines within an area** by copying an existing row. The formula comes along.
      4. **Set the contingency** at the bottom. Most productions carry between 5 and 10%; [how much contingency a production needs](/insights/how-much-contingency-does-a-production-need/) explains what it is for and who gets to release it.
      5. **Save a copy per version.** When the schedule changes, compare the old and the new file in the [budget comparison tool](/tools/budget-compare/) and see exactly what moved.

      - question: Which currency does it use?
        answer: |
          None is set. Type amounts in whatever currency you budget in, and add the currency to the header row if you share the file. The number format shows two decimals with thousands separators, which Excel adapts to your regional settings.
      - question: Can I add, remove or rename lines?
        answer: |
          Yes, it is a normal spreadsheet. Copy a row to add a line within an area (the formula comes along), delete the rows you do not need, and rename anything. If you add a whole new area, copy the highlighted header row and check that the area's range on the Summary by Category sheet includes the new lines.
      - question: Where are fringes and payroll costs?
        answer: |
          The template does not calculate fringes separately, because the rates differ per country, union and contract. Add them as a line in the area they belong to, or as a percentage row at the bottom next to contingency: copy the contingency row and change the label and the percentage.
