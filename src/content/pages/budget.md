  - type: feature
    theme: light
    heading: What is in it
    text: |
      - **Your own templates and price lists.** Every producer builds the same way, and a camera day costs the same everywhere.
      - **Globals.** Numbers such as episodes, shoot days or a day rate are defined once and used in every line that needs them.
      - **Versions.** Save each version with a name, go back to any of them, and lock the one the client agreed to.
      - **Compare.** Put budgets side by side and see the difference per category and per line.
      - **AI first draft.** Describe the production and let AI suggest lines, quantities and prices in your own cost structure. You check it line by line.
      - **Import from Movie Magic Budgeting.** Upload its Excel export and every detail line comes in.
      - **Cost and sales.** For service productions and commercials, set a margin per line or per group and see the quote total next to your cost.
      - **Quotations and budget documents.** From your own templates, sent as a PDF or signed digitally.
      - **A secure link for stakeholders.** A client, financier or co-producer can read along and comment on a line, without a Tubes account.
      - **Series and episodes.** Budget per episode and, where it matters, per minute.
      - **Sign-off.** Name who has to approve a budget and see who still has to.
      - **Excel export.** Every budget exports to Excel or CSV.
