      - question: Can I book these locations through Tubes?
        answer: |
          No. This guide is free and independent: every entry links to the official site of the location or studio and to the film commission that covers it. You deal with them directly. Tubes is production management software, not a booking service.
      - question: How do I get my location or studio listed?
        answer: |
          Use the "Suggest a location" form on this page. Tell us what and where it is, add a link, and confirm you are entitled to submit it. Listing is free. Photos must be yours or come with a licence we can honour, with the photographer credited.
      - question: Where do the photos come from?
        answer: |
          From Wikimedia Commons under a free licence, with the photographer and licence named on every photo, or from owners who gave permission. We never copy photos from other location databases.
      - question: How were these locations selected?
        answer: |
          The starting set is hand-picked and verified: places with a documented production history and studios that productions can actually hire, a few per country, checked in August 2026. The guide grows through suggestions, so the selection is a starting point, not a ranking.
      - question: What about the production incentive in each country?
