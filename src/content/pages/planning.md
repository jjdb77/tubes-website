    text: |
      The budget is approved. The production starts. Crew have to be booked, locations confirmed, props and equipment arranged, and all of it has to land on the right days.

      On most productions that lives in a few places at once. A spreadsheet for the schedule, a chat group for the crew, an email thread per location. It works, until a location turns out to be free a week later or the DOP is only available for part of the shoot, and everything has to be moved by hand.

      In Tubes you put people, locations and props on the days of the production, and everyone looks at the same plan. And while you plan, you also see where the budget stands.

  - type: feature
    theme: light
    heading: What is in it
    text: |
      - **Four ways to look at the plan.** A timeline of the production, a board where you drag people, locations and props onto days, and a calendar per week or per month.
      - **Drag to reschedule.** Move a day to another date, or stretch it over the days that follow.
      - **Everything a day needs.** Cast and crew with their own call times, locations, props and equipment, and files.
      - **Draft until you publish.** Change a published day and Tubes marks it as changed.
      - **Weather on the day.** The forecast per day and location, on the plan and on the call sheet.
      - **Capacity and workload.** Planned days against available days, and one person's load across all productions.
      - **Locations you can compare.** Facilities, photos and a price guide per location, up to three side by side.
      - **From script to shooting days.** Paste or upload a script, let Tubes split it into scenes and group them into shooting days.
      - **On the phone.** Crew with a Tubes account see their published days and call sheets, and can confirm there.
      - **See where the budget stands.** Budget, planned and what is left are in view while you plan.

  - type: faq
    theme: light
    heading: Questions about planning in Tubes
    items:
      - question: Can we use planning on its own?
        answer: |
          No. Planning is part of the full platform (Producer Pro and Enterprise) and is not sold separately. What you can do is [start with budgeting only](/budgeting/) and expand to the full platform later, in the same account.
      - question: Does the crew need a Tubes account?
        answer: |
          Not to confirm. Each person gets a personal link that works without logging in. They see the day and answer that they will be there or that they cannot make it. Crew who do have a Tubes account also see their published days and call sheets on their phone.
      - question: How do call sheets reach the crew?
        answer: |
          By email from Tubes, per department, with the call sheet as a PDF, a calendar file and a personal confirmation link. You can also copy someone's confirmation link and send it yourself, for instance in a chat message.
      - question: What happens when the plan changes after it was sent?
        answer: |
          A published day that you edit is marked as changed. Per person you see whether they have seen the day, confirmed it or declined it, so you know who still has to react to the new version.
      - question: What does it cost?
        answer: |
          Planning is included in Producer Pro, from € 49 per month including two users and up to three active projects, and in Enterprise. See [plans and pricing](/plans/).
