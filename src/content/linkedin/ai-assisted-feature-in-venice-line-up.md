Be Brave, an 85 minute portrait of Diesel founder Renzo Rosso, was built with AI tools and archive material and has a place in the Venice Film Festival line-up. The interesting part for producers is not the novelty. It is what a film like this says about where production cost, virtual production and creative direction are heading.
