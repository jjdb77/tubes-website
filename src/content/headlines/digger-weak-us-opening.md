Digger, the new film by Alejandro González Iñárritu with Tom Cruise, opened in the United States on 2 October in more than 3,300 cinemas. On its first Friday it took about 3.4 million dollars, 1.2 million of which came from previews, according to The Numbers.

The first estimates put the North American opening weekend at around 8 million dollars. If that holds, it is the lowest opening for a Cruise film since Lions for Lambs in 2007. These are provisional figures.

The production budget is reported at around 125 million dollars, before marketing. Warner Bros. releases the film in cinemas and IMAX. In the Netherlands it opened a day earlier, on 1 October.

A weak opening weekend does not settle whether a film loses money. International box office, a longer run, streaming rights and TV sales all count towards the final result. Digger is an original story rather than part of a franchise, which is why its start is watched closely in a market where studios mostly bet on familiar titles.
