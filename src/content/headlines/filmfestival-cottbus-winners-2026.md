FilmFestival Cottbus announced the prizes for its 36th edition on Sunday. The top prize went to You Don’t Belong Here by Romanian director Florin Șerban, once again giving the festival an international platform for cinema from Central and Eastern Europe.

The jury awarded You Don’t Belong Here the prize for best film. Film New Europe reported the result on Sunday; the film is directed by Florin Șerban. It is not known whether the prize includes prize money or distribution deals.

For producers and sales agents, Cottbus is particularly relevant as a gateway to markets beyond the major Western European festival circuits. The festival focuses on cinema from Central and Eastern Europe and links screenings to industry activities and international co-production contacts.

The announcement came several days after the prizes at Connecting Cottbus, the co-production market linked to the festival. Cineuropa reported, among other things, awards for projects titled Tongues, Svetlana and This Is It, the Apocalypse. Those market awards are separate from the festival’s top prize.

The result could give the winning production additional visibility among distributors and festivals. That does not automatically mean that a release in the Netherlands, Belgium or Germany has been secured; public reporting contains no confirmation of this.

The award in Cottbus could lead to talks with European programmers, funds and sales companies. The business consequences of the honour are not yet known.
