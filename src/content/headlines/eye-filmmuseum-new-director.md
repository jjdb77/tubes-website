Emily Ansenk will become director and chief executive of Eye Filmmuseum on 1 February 2027. She will succeed Bregtje van der Haak, who left in the spring; interim director John Middelweerd will remain in post until Ansenk arrives.

Eye’s supervisory board appointed Ansenk following a selection procedure. Ansenk has led the Holland Festival since 2019. Before that, she was director of Kunsthal Rotterdam and the Scheringa Museum for Realism.

Ansenk says she wants to focus on Eye’s artistic direction, strategy and organisational challenges.

The appointment follows a period of reorganisation and financial pressure at Eye. Van der Haak left in April 2026 after a restructuring was carried out under her leadership. Middelweerd has held the position on an interim basis since then.

According to the announcement, the supervisory board sees Ansenk as an executive with experience in both museums and festivals. Her career has included public programming, fundraising, education and the development of new cultural activities.

Ansenk says she wants to use her experience for Eye’s artistic direction, strategy and organisational challenges. She describes the collection and the museum’s two buildings as important starting points for the period ahead.

The new director will not start for several months. Middelweerd was appointed interim director. Ansenk herself identifies the artistic direction, strategy and organisation as areas of focus.
