Dutch distributor Skoop Media launched the Dutch Noir label during the international television market Mipcom. The selection focuses on crime series, thrillers and heist stories, and aims to bring Dutch productions more prominently to international attention.

Dutch Noir is, according to Skoop Media, a new label for high-quality Dutch crime series. According to reports on the presentation, the opening selection consists of seventeen titles from six producers, including four new Videoland Originals. Skoop describes the label as a way to offer different series under one recognisable international umbrella.

The core of the selection consists of productions from the Mocro Maffia universe. The distributor also sells series such as Sleepers, Tonnano and other Dutch thrillers. On its own website, Skoop describes its catalogue as comprising more than sixteen crime series, ten of which have multiple seasons.

The launch is taking place during Mipcom in Cannes, an international trade fair where broadcasters, streaming services, producers and distributors buy and sell programmes. The fair runs from 12 to 15 October. Such markets are important for productions that must recoup their costs through more than just a Dutch broadcaster or streaming service.

Skoop says Dutch crime stories have already been sold in several regions, including Europe, Latin America and Japan. The distributor does not provide a complete list of sales, viewing figures or comparable market data. It is therefore not possible to establish independently whether Dutch crime series are genuinely more popular worldwide than ever before.

It is clear, however, that Dutch producers and distributors are trying to organise international sales more professionally. A separate label can help foreign buyers recognise titles more quickly and can give producers access to joint promotion. It can also reinforce the impression that Dutch series are associated primarily with gritty crime and thrillers.

International interest does not change the makers’ responsibility for the content. Series about organised crime can give foreign viewers an impression of the Netherlands, but remain fiction. Whether Dutch Noir develops into a sustainable export brand will depend on actual sales, viewing figures and follow-up productions, not just on its launch at Mipcom.
