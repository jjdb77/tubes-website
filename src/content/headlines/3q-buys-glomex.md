German video technology companies 3Q and glomex are to be combined. 3Q is acquiring glomex from ProSiebenSat.1; completion is expected in the first quarter of 2027.

Glomex will remain as a brand and become a wholly owned subsidiary of 3Q. Lars Friedrichs will remain glomex's managing director, according to the announcement. The financial terms of the transaction have not been disclosed.

3Q provides infrastructure for video distribution and, according to ProSiebenSat.1, works with more than 1,000 corporate customers in Europe. The company sells its platform, among other ways, as software-as-a-service on a licensing basis.

Glomex adds a marketplace for video content, content syndication and monetisation solutions. Glomex's existing revenue model is based on revenue-sharing with publishers and content partners.

For ProSiebenSat.1, the sale forms part of the previously announced winding-down of activities outside the entertainment company's core business. The announcement does not state a price, nor does it explain the implications of the sale for existing contracts with publishers.

The combination shifts glomex's position from a platform within a large media group to a specialised European infrastructure and distribution company. What this means for publishers will depend on its future services.

The transaction has not yet closed. The precise operational and commercial implications therefore remain uncertain until the expected closing in the first quarter of 2027.
