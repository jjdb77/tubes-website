Truly Naked was the big winner of the 46th Netherlands Film Festival in Utrecht. Muriel d'Ansembourg's film won the Golden Calves for best feature film, best direction and best screenplay, and also for cinematography, editing and production design. That is six of the seven categories it was nominated in.

Daan Buringa won best leading role in a feature film for Joe Speedboot. Thor Braun took best supporting role for Downtown, the festival's opening film, which also won the award for costume design.

In the series categories the drama BASTA stood out, with best series and best direction. Chris Peters won best leading role in a series.

In total 26 Golden Calves were presented in 25 categories, covering feature films, documentaries, series and digital culture. The awards are recognition from within the Dutch film sector and say nothing in themselves about audience numbers or box office.
