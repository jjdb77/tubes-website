The SBS6 programme De Bondgenoten has won the 2026 Golden Televizier-Ring. Presenter Jan Versteegh also received the award for best presenter during the gala at Koninklijk Theater Carré in Amsterdam.

The result was announced on Thursday evening during the 61st edition of the Golden Televizier-Ring Gala. According to public reports, De Bondgenoten defeated the other nominated programmes and received the public award for the most popular television programme.

The presenter award also went to SBS6. Jan Versteegh beat Tim Hofman and Thomas van Groningen. This is not Versteegh’s first Televizier award: he already won the prize for rising talent in 2015.

De Bondgenoten is a reality programme in which participants carry out tasks in teams and compete for a cash prize. Versteegh has presented the programme since its launch in 2023. It has therefore become a regular part of SBS6’s programming.

The award confirms the visibility of reality television among the most important Dutch public awards. The winner of the Televizier-Ring is chosen by viewers, meaning the result mainly says something about popularity and audience engagement, rather than constituting an independent assessment of content.

For SBS6, the victory also has commercial significance. A public award can increase a programme’s value to advertisers, channel promotion and subsequent seasons. The result also underlines the position of Talpa’s reality offering in a television market in which linear channels compete with streaming services.

The victory is therefore more than a gala talking point. It shows which programmes currently have a strong direct connection with viewers. At the same time, the result says nothing about viewing figures, production costs or how long reality formats will continue to retain their audience.
