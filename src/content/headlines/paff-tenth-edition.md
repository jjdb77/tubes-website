The Amsterdam festival opens with Cristian Mungiu's Palme d'Or winner Fjord and closes with Jordan Firstman's Club Kid, which premiered in Un Certain Regard in Cannes. In between are new films by Hamaguchi, Almodóvar, Dhont and Pawlikowski.
