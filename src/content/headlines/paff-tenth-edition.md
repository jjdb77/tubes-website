Paff, the Amsterdam festival for auteur cinema, closes its tenth edition with Club Kid by Jordan Firstman, who wrote and directed the film and plays the lead. Club Kid had its world premiere in Un Certain Regard in Cannes and was nominated there for the Caméra d'Or and the Queer Palm.

The festival opens with Fjord by Cristian Mungiu, this year's Palme d'Or winner. In between are new films by Ryusuke Hamaguchi, Pedro Almodóvar, Lukas Dhont, Pawel Pawlikowski and Rudolf van den Berg, plus an anniversary screening of Amélie.

Screenings take place in several Amsterdam cinemas, including Het Ketelhuis, Cinecenter, FC Hyena, FilmHallen, Kriterion and Rialto de Pijp. Tickets are on sale through the festival website and the cinemas.

For the Dutch sector Paff is mainly a public platform for international arthouse films that do not all get the same room in regular cinema programming. Most of the line-up has already travelled through the big festivals this year.
