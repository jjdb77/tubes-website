Netflix will launch its subscription with adverts in the Netherlands and Belgium on 1 March 2027. It announced the date at an event for advertisers. The two countries are part of a rollout to fifteen new markets next year, including Austria, Denmark, Ireland, Norway, Poland, Sweden and Switzerland.

The price for the Netherlands and Belgium has not been announced. Existing subscriptions are not replaced automatically. On the ad plan, breaks appear before and during many films and series, and Netflix says a small part of the catalogue may be missing because of licence agreements.

Netflix also brings its own advertising technology, the Netflix Ads Suite, to the Dutch market. That lets advertisers buy, target and measure campaigns directly with Netflix, which makes it a competitor for the advertising budgets that now go to broadcasters.

How many adverts Dutch viewers will see, which titles are left out and whether the ad-free prices change is not known yet.
