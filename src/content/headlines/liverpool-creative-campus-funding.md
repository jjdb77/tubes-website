The UK government is reserving £20 million for the restoration and redevelopment of the former Littlewoods site in Liverpool, subject to approval of a final business case. The project is intended to provide space for film, television, gaming, immersive technology and digital screen industries.

The contribution comes from the Growth Mission Fund and is included in the UK’s 2026 Budget documents. The money is intended for a creative campus of approximately 260,000 square feet on the site. The funding is subject to approval of a final business case.

The Littlewoods project is intended to become a film and television complex within a broader creative development. Alongside production space, the plans include facilities for gaming, immersive technology and other digital screen activities. The project therefore targets both production infrastructure and Liverpool’s wider creative economy.

The development has a long history. According to BBC reporting, discussions about repurposing the former building have been ongoing since 2017. The BBC reported that Twickenham Studios had previously been announced as the anchor tenant, but had withdrawn from the project.

The new government contribution is intended to get the project moving again, but does not guarantee that the campus will be built immediately. Both the requirement for a final business case and the previous delays remain commercial risks for the developer, public funders and future tenants.

The Liverpool Film Office points to a lack of additional space for productions. The Liverpool Film Office says productions are coming to the city, but that there are insufficient workshops, support facilities and innovation space available. A new campus could increase that capacity, but the funding remains subject to approval of a final business case.

The decision fits with UK policy to strengthen regional creative clusters outside London. Liverpool could become more attractive to producers if permanent studios, post-production, training and facilities support become available at one location. For now, it remains a financial commitment; no opening date has been announced.
