BBC Studios made BBC Player available as a Prime Video channel in Spain and Poland on 12 November; Switzerland will follow on 3 December, according to the announcement. The expansion builds on the channel’s existing availability within Prime Video in Germany and Austria.

BBC Studios describes BBC Player as a paid video-on-demand service from BBC Studios, offering series, documentaries and other programmes from the BBC catalogue. In the new markets, the service is offered as a standalone channel within Prime Video, meaning viewers do not need to use a separate BBC Player app to access the subscription.

The launch in Spain and Poland took place on 12 November. For Switzerland, BBC Studios gives 3 December as the launch date. The company says the expansion builds on BBC Player’s availability within Prime Video in Germany and Austria.

This enables BBC Studios to offer BBC content through a single international platform in multiple European markets. For Amazon, the partnership provides an additional paid channel option within Prime Video. For BBC Studios, the model could limit distribution costs and local technical complexity, although the company has not published financial terms on this.

The first independent reports cite an offering of more than 1,000 hours of BBC content in Spain and Poland. The exact catalogue and price may vary by country. A Spanish trade publication gives a price of €3.99 per month, but BBC Studios does not mention that price in the primary announcement; it is therefore not presented here as a general European price.

The expansion does not mean that BBC programmes are available exclusively through Prime Video everywhere. The channel model in itself does not rule out BBC Player also being offered within other distribution environments, or BBC Studios working with other providers in Spain. The Prime Video channel may offer benefits in terms of reach, billing and discoverability among existing platform users.

For European broadcasters and distributors, the move is an example of using channel models within major streaming platforms. A service can be rolled out internationally without building a fully independent platform in every country. The trade-off is that the provider remains dependent on the host platform’s search function, subscription terms and commercial position.

BBC Studios has published no subscription targets or expected revenues. Nor has it disclosed what local marketing investments are being made. The new launch is therefore primarily a distribution and market expansion, rather than a publicly disclosed forecast for BBC Player’s success.
