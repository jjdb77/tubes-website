The Dutch market for pay TV and online video grew by 2.5 per cent in the second quarter of 2026, to 767 million euros, according to research by Telecompaper. The figure covers revenue, not viewing time.

Cable and telecom providers still take 56 per cent of that revenue, but their share is slowly falling. Streaming services that sell directly to viewers account for about 27 per cent. Broadcasters and their own video services make up 16 per cent, and that group grew fastest, mostly thanks to Videoland.

Paying per film is losing ground. According to Totaal TV, transactional video on demand fell by 29 per cent in the quarter, while services combine subscriptions, advertising and one-off payments for films or sport.

More change may follow. Videoland has asked the Dutch competition authority ACM for permission to take sole control of Viaplay Nederland. The notification was published in August, and the authority has not decided yet. For producers it means the main Dutch buyers of series and films are increasingly the services that sell straight to viewers.
