Videoland, the Dutch streaming service of DPG Media, adds audio and video podcasts from 8 October. It brings programmes, podcast brands and creators from the RTL and Videoland networks together in one place.

The first titles include Niet Voor Uitzending with Renze Klamer, Tom van 't Einde and Gijs Rademaker, and an audio version of the talk show Renze. Moordcast moves to Videoland exclusively from 23 October, and Bankzitters de Podcast also becomes exclusive. RTL Nieuws is working on a daily news podcast, and Videoland is developing its own titles in news, entertainment and true crime.

Ad Alliance sells the advertising around the podcasts and video podcasts. Videoland also wants to add existing DPG titles and work with new creators and podcast brands.

Videoland has not said how many subscribers or how much revenue it expects from the move, or which podcasts stay available elsewhere. It does show streamers competing for creators and daily attention, not only for viewing time, and it adds a new kind of commission for producers who make talk and documentary formats.
