From 2027 the Primetime Emmy Awards will be streamed live on Amazon Prime Video, in more than 240 countries and territories. The Television Academy signed an exclusive deal for six years. The first ceremony under the new contract is planned for September 2027.

Since 1955 the Emmys have been shown by one of the big American networks, and for decades ABC, CBS, Fox and NBC took turns. That arrangement now ends. According to Amazon and the Academy, the ceremony stays free to watch, so viewers will not need a Prime subscription.

The Academy says it wanted one partner that can build on the show year after year. Amazon gets a large live event about the series and the people who make them, including series from its competitors.

The Academy keeps running the awards and the nominations. What changes is where the show is found and who promotes it. How free access will work in each country, and what Amazon will produce around the ceremony, has not been worked out in detail yet.
