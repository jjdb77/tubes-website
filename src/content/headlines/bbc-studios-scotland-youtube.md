BBC Studios has launched the five-part travel series Scran & Scramble: An American in Scotland on its own YouTube channel The Travel Edit. The series pairs an American creator with a Scottish guide and is released weekly.

The first episode appeared on 23 October. The series consists of five 22-minute episodes and follows American creator Kobie Jordan and Scottish creator and guide Bryan Millar Walker on a road trip through Scotland.

BBC Studios Digital Brands developed and commissioned the project. Production is handled by Aurora Media Worldwide, part of Banijay Entertainment. New episodes appear on Saturdays on The Travel Edit, a BBC Studios channel for travel, adventure and food content.

The format uses the two makers’ different perspectives as its editorial starting point. Jordan brings expectations shaped in part by film and television; Walker adds local knowledge and access to Scottish locations.

The route includes Edinburgh, Ben Nevis and Mangersta Beach, among others. The series also features a hotel stay, an animal rescue centre, local music, castles, fly-fishing and curling. It is therefore structured as a combination of a personal creator story and a classic travel documentary.

For BBC Studios, the launch fits a distribution strategy in which programmes are not made exclusively for linear television or an owned streaming service. YouTube offers direct international distribution and makes it possible to connect a creator audience with an established broadcaster.

The available information gives no viewing figures, revenue model or advertising arrangements. Nor is it known whether the series will later be offered on other BBC platforms or by external broadcasters in addition to YouTube. The commercial development therefore lies primarily in the production and distribution model, rather than in an announced sales deal.
