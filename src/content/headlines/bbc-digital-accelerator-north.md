The BBC and screen agency North East Screen are jointly distributing £400,000 among four production companies in North East England. The support is part of the Digital Accelerator programme, which North East Screen says aims to develop new digital content for a young online audience.

The selected companies are Adventure Alliance Films and Northern Child from Newcastle, FilmNova from Gateshead and Wander Films from Middlesbrough. Each company will receive £100,000, North East Screen reported in its announcement of 6 November.

The programme focuses on digital productions that can be developed and distributed outside traditional television. The precise titles and formats have not yet been announced. The BBC and North East Screen present the investment as support for content that reflects younger audiences’ media habits.

North East Screen also positions the scheme as regional sector development. The screen agency wants to promote the region not only as a production location, but also as a place where makers can test new ways of producing and distributing content and building audiences.

The scheme is relevant to the UK independent production landscape because the funding is not going to a single national producer, but to four companies outside London. This links development capital to regional infrastructure and digital distribution channels.

The contribution is no guarantee of a long-term BBC commission. It does, however, reduce the development and pilot risk for the four companies, while giving the BBC access to formats designed for online use from the outset.

For European producers, the combination of public funding, regional sector policy and platform-focused production is particularly interesting. The model shows how broadcasters are trying to reach young audiences without simply moving existing television formats to online channels.
