Avid has announced Ready to Play, which the company describes as an optional extension for Media Composer. The service uses models from Google, including Gemini and Veo, for three workflows: creating video, modifying video and analysing video material.

According to Avid, Ready to Play was developed as part of its expanded collaboration with Google Cloud. Avid says the features work with clips, bins and metadata already present in Media Composer. It therefore positions the service as an extension of the editing workflow, rather than as a standalone generative video application.

The first three features each have a different purpose. Create Video focuses on generating video material, Modify Video on adapting existing material, and Analyse Video on searching and interpreting media and metadata.

For newsrooms and post-production companies, the integration with existing project structures is particularly relevant. If material, metadata and generated results remain within the same environment, this could reduce the number of handovers between editing software and external AI services.

The announcement does not say which models are used by default for each feature, how source material is stored or what rights arrangement applies to generated images. Nor are independent performance measurements or concrete workflow tests for large post-production operations available in the public product information.

The launch follows earlier announcements by Avid and Google Cloud about browser-based Media Composer and so-called agentic workflows. According to Avid, Ready to Play is the first in a series of new AI offerings within that collaboration.

For European facilities companies, the technical assessment therefore extends beyond the demonstration of features. Security, data location, logging, permission from rights holders, human oversight and integration with existing storage and archive systems will determine whether the extension can be used in professional productions.
