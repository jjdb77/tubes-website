## Audit logs

Tubes keeps a field-level history of finance transactions: who changed which field, from what to what, and when. Open it from a transaction, or under **Settings > Audit logs** for the whole account. Approval decisions and export batches are logged too.

Use it when a financier asks why an amount changed, or when an invoice ended up on the wrong cost type and you want to know when.

## Automations

**Settings > Automations** lets you define what Tubes does when something happens: for example, notify a channel when a budget is published, or move a project to the next status when its quotation is signed. Each automation has a trigger, conditions and an action.

## Translation debug reports

When a quotation was translated with AI, the debug report shows the source and the result side by side, so you can check a translation before it goes out.

## Support access

For support, Tubes staff can be given temporary access to your account. You see it as "Support access" and can end it at any time.
