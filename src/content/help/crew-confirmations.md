When you send a call sheet, every person on it receives their own confirmation link. It works without a Tubes account.

## What the crew sees

The email asks "Can you make it?" with two buttons: **Yes, I'll be there** and **I can't make it**. The link opens a small page that records the answer, and the same link can be used later to change it. Links work for 30 days; after that the planner can send a new one.

## What the planner sees

In the activity panel, each person shows **Pending** or **Confirmed**. When everyone has answered, the day shows "confirmed". A decline is noted so you can find a replacement.

## Send a link by hand

Next to a person in the day you can copy their confirmation link, valid 30 days and no login needed, to send it by WhatsApp or another channel.

> **Tip:** Confirmations are per person per day. Duplicating a day does not copy the confirmations; the new day starts as pending.
