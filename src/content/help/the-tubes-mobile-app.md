The Tubes mobile app is for everyone on a production who needs the schedule and the call sheet on their phone, not the full workspace.

## Sign in

Sign in with the same account as on the web. If you only received call sheets so far and have no account, ask the production to invite you.

## What is in the app

- **Home**: what is on today and what comes next.
- **Schedule**: your upcoming days as a list or a calendar, this week and later.
- **My Schedule**: your own days, with a button to confirm each day.
- **Callsheets**: the published call sheets you are on, with cast & crew, locations, scenes and specifications. Call sheets appear here when they are published.
- **Production log**: add a remark during the day, for example a late arrival, a change of location or a piece of equipment that failed. The production office sees it in Tubes.
- **Chat**: the conversation of the production.
- **Settings**: your profile, and the option to delete your account.

## What is not in the app

Budgets, finance and approvals are web only. Actions from a call sheet, such as changing the schedule, are read-only in the app; ask the planner.
