## The trial

A new account starts as a **Trial**. You have the full workspace for the trial period. Tubes warns you in the days before it ends: "Your trial ends in (n) days. Choose a subscription now to keep access to Tubes."

## When the trial ends

Without a subscription the account becomes **Read-only**: you can sign in and look at everything, but not change it. "Your trial has ended. Choose a subscription to keep using Tubes." Choose a plan under **Billing** and the account is active again with all your data.

## Billing statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Trial | The trial period. |
| Paid | An active subscription. |
| Past due | A payment failed. Update the payment method to continue. |
| Read-only | No active subscription; the account can be read but not changed. |
| Cancelled | The subscription was cancelled. |
| Deactivated | The account was closed. |

## Failed payment

When a card is declined the status becomes **Past due**: "Your payment method was declined. Please add another payment method to continue." Update it under **Billing > Payment Method**.

## Cancel

You can cancel at any time; there are no hidden costs. The account then becomes read-only at the end of the paid period. Export what you need first: budgets to Excel, transactions in batches, documents as PDF. See [Export budgets and reports](/help/imports-exports/export-budgets-and-reports/).
