## The plans

- **Producer Pro** starts from € 49 per month and includes 2 users and up to 3 active projects. Each additional user is € 25 per month, up to 10 users. "Active" means running at the same time, not per year.
- **Enterprise** is for larger teams: unlimited projects, more users, integrations with finance, payroll and planning tools, and an agreement on paper. [Book a call](/book-a-call/enterprise/) to discuss it.
- **Tubes Projects** is priced per production and includes 5 users; add users in blocks of 5, pro-rated for the remaining duration. Ask us about it.

Prices are shown on the [plans page](/plans/) in your local currency.

## Choose a plan

1. Open **Account > Billing**, or the **Payment** step during setup.
2. Choose a plan and a billing period: monthly, half year or annual.
3. Add a payment method and confirm. By confirming you allow Tubes to charge your payment method for this and future payments.

## Change plan or period

Under **Billing** you can switch plan or billing period at any time; the difference is settled pro rata.

## Payment method

Update your card under **Billing > Payment Method**. Some accounts have free access and do not need a payment method; Billing says so.

## Users and projects beyond your plan

A user beyond the included number is charged per month as an extra unit. A project beyond the maximum of the plan is not possible until you archive one or move to a plan with more projects. Ask Tubes when you are close to the limit.
