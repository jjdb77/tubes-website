A client, a co-producer or a financier does not need a Tubes account to look at a budget. You share it with a magic link.

## Share

1. Open the budget and choose **Share budget via magic link**.
2. Enter the email address of the person and send.

They receive an email "Budget shared: (name)". When they open the link, Tubes sends a verification code to the same email address; they enter the code and see the budget read-only. Links are valid for a limited number of weeks and the panel shows when each link was last viewed.

## Comments

The reader can leave comments on the shared budget. You see them in Tubes, so questions about a line can be answered where the line is.

## Revoke

In the share panel, the **Active magic links** list shows who has access. Choose **Revoke** to end a link immediately.

> **Note:** A magic link shows the budget as it is at the moment of viewing. If you want the reader to see a fixed version, share a published budget or send a quotation instead.
