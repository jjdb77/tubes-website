A quotation turns a budget into a priced proposal for your client. Pick a budget, choose what to show, and send it out for approval or signing.

## Create

1. Open the budget and choose **Documents**, or go to **Quotations** in the sidebar and choose **New quotation**.
2. Choose the budget the quotation is based on and a quotation template.
3. Decide what the client sees: the level of detail (totals per phase, per category, or every line), the sales prices, the additional costs and the discounts.
4. Write or load the text: an introduction, the assumptions, the terms. Templates can hold default texts and reusable snippets, and you can add sections of your own.
5. Save. The quotation is a **Draft**.

## Send and follow

Print the quotation as PDF or send it from Tubes. Its status moves from Draft to **Sent**, and to **Signed** when the client has signed, or **Expired** when the validity date passes. An administrator can add statuses under **Settings > Quotation statuses**.

## Signing

Switch **Signing** on for a quotation to have the client sign it digitally through DocuSign or DocuSeal. The signed document is returned to the project. See [Digital signing with DocuSign or DocuSeal](/help/portal/digital-signing/).

## Translate a quotation

Tubes can translate the text of a quotation into another language with AI. The translation is a job that runs in the background; check the result before sending, and keep a debug report if something looks off.

## Quotation numbers

A quotation gets a number when you give it one; the field is optional. Use your own numbering or let Tubes count.

> **Tip:** A quotation is a document derived from the budget. Change the budget and create a new quotation rather than editing amounts in the quotation, so both always match.
