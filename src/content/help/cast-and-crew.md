**Cast & crew** is the list of people you plan on productions: your own staff, freelancers and cast.

## A person

A person has a name, contact details, roles and skills (so you can filter crew lanes on "sound" or "gaffer"), and the rates and cost allocations that determine how planning a day on them affects the budget.

## Availability

Assignment agreements say when a person is **Available**, on **Contract**, on **Leave**, **Unavailable** or on **Vacation**, with dates. The planning uses this: hatched days in the crew lanes are days off, and the capacity view counts available days.

## Import

Import a crew list from a file instead of typing it. The import shows a preview and a mapping of the columns before it runs, and can be undone.

## On a production

Within a project, **Cast & Crew**, **Casting** and **Recruitment** under **Production** show who is on this production, in which role, and who is still needed. A person planned on a day appears on the call sheet and gets a confirmation link. See [Crew confirmations](/help/planning/crew-confirmations/).

## Payroll

Employer costs for your own staff can be uploaded as payroll so they land on the productions as cost. Ask your finance team, or Tubes, about the format.
