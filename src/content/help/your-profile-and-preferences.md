## Profile

Open **Account > Profile** for your name, email address, avatar, timezone and language. Tubes is available in English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Swedish and Hungarian; documents you send use the language of the template, not your interface language.

## Password and security

**Password & Security** is where you change your password and manage two-factor authentication. See [Sign in, two-factor authentication and your session](/help/getting-started/sign-in-and-security/).

## Connected accounts

**Connected Accounts** lists the Google, Apple, Microsoft or GitHub accounts you can sign in with. Connect or disconnect them here.

## Notifications

The bell in the sidebar (**Updates**) collects your notifications: an accepted invitation, an ownership transfer, a transaction stuck without approvers. **Mark all as read** clears them. **What's New** holds the release notes.

## Preferences

**Preferences** at the bottom of the sidebar: the layout (Classic or Concept), which sidebar items you hide for yourself, and the planning mode (simple or advanced) that is remembered per member.

![Preferences with the two sidebar layouts](/assets/images/app-preferences.jpg)

## Accounts

If you belong to more than one Tubes account, **Accounts** lets you switch, and shows the role of each workspace.

## Delete your account

You can delete your own user account from the mobile app under Settings, or ask the owner of the workspace. Productions and transactions you worked on stay in the account.
