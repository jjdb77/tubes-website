While you are building, a budget is a concept: editable, with saved versions for your own history. When the budget is agreed, you publish it.

## Publish

1. Open the budget and choose **Publication**.
2. Check the totals and choose **Publish**.

A published budget is locked and creates a formal version record ("Publication v1"). It is what the production team and the forecast work from. To change a published budget, edit the concept and publish again: the new publication replaces the current one and the previous one moves to the **Publication History**. From that history you can restore an earlier version as the current publication.

## Work budget

Production rarely runs exactly as budgeted. The work budget is a copy of the publication that production can edit, without touching the published version that was agreed with the client.

- Create it from the publication screen with **Create Work Budget**. This is a one-time action for the budget.
- The work budget is based on the current publication and is **not** updated automatically when you publish a new version. The screen tells you when the work budget is based on an older publication than the current one. Any changes must be made by hand.
- Work budget lines are what the forecast measures against: cost to date, committed costs and estimate to complete are compared with the work budget. See [Cost to date, committed costs and EFC](/help/reporting/cost-to-date-committed-and-efc/).

Approving a budget (via its status) can create the work budget in the same step; removing the approval removes it again.

> **Note:** Whether the work budget feature is available for an account is set by Tubes. Contact us if you do not see it.

## Release to planning

From the budget toolbar, **Plan this budget** turns the activities of the budget into the base planning of the production. You give them their days, people, items and locations in the planning. See [How planning works](/help/planning/how-planning-works/).
