## Where is my data stored?

On EU-based cloud services in Amsterdam, with daily backups, GDPR-aligned data handling and clear data ownership per account. See [Security & Compliance](/compliance/).

## Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. There are no hidden costs. The account becomes read-only at the end of the paid period, and you can export your budgets, transactions and documents before that. See [Trial, subscription and billing status](/help/billing/trial-subscription-and-billing-status/).

## Can I start small and expand later?

Yes. Teams often begin with budgeting and quotations, and add planning, cost control and reporting later. The screens are there from day one; you use what you need.

## Which languages does Tubes have?

English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Swedish and Hungarian. Set yours under **Account > Profile**.

## Does Tubes work on my phone?

The web app works in a mobile browser, and there is a mobile app for schedules, call sheets, confirmations and chat. See [The Tubes mobile app](/help/planning/the-tubes-mobile-app/).

## Do my crew need an account?

No. Call sheets go out by email with a PDF, a calendar file and a personal confirmation link that needs no login. Members are only needed for people who work inside Tubes.

## Can I import my Movie Magic budgets?

Yes, as JSON (complete) or Excel (totals per category). See [Import a Movie Magic budget](/help/imports-exports/import-a-movie-magic-budget/).

## Does Tubes connect to my accounting system?

Xero and Exact are supported for exporting approved costs; every export is also available as Excel. See [Connect Xero](/help/imports-exports/connect-xero/).

## Who can see the budget?

Members with the right role. Outsiders can get a read-only view by magic link with a verification code, or a quotation. Never through the portal.

## How do I get help?

Ask the **Help** assistant in the sidebar, send **Feedback**, or email [contact@tubes.media](mailto:contact@tubes.media). See [Get help inside Tubes](/help/getting-started/get-help-inside-tubes/).
