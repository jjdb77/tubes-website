The portal is the outside entrance of Tubes, for people who are not on your team but do have business with a production: a client who wants to follow the project, a co-producer, a supplier who sends invoices.

## Signing in

The portal has no passwords. A visitor enters their email address and receives a login link, valid for 60 minutes. If the address is known to you as a contact with portal access, the link opens their portal: "Welcome to the portal of (your company)".

## What a visitor sees

- **Your projects**: the projects they are linked to, with status and the documents you shared.
- **Submit invoice**: upload an invoice for a project, or without a project. See [Submit an invoice through the portal](/help/portal/submit-an-invoice/).

A visitor only sees the projects they are linked to. Budgets, costs and internal notes are never shown in the portal.

## Giving access

Open the contact under **Contacts** and give them portal access. Revoke it there too; a revoked visitor sees "Your access has been revoked" when they try the link.

> **Note:** A budget shared by magic link is something else: a read-only view of one budget, with a verification code. See [Share a budget with someone outside Tubes](/help/budgeting/share-a-budget-outside-tubes/).
