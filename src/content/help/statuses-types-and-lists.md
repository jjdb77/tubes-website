Much of the vocabulary in Tubes is a list you can change. **Settings** groups them.

## Types, statuses and categories

- **Budget statuses** (Concept, Active, Approved, Completed, Cancelled) and **Budget types**.
- **Project statuses** and **Project types** (commercial, documentary, drama series, live).
- **Client statuses** and **Client types**.
- **Quotation statuses** (Draft, Sent, Signed, Expired).
- **Financing categories** (Lead Producer(s), Co-producers, Broadcaster Contribution, Subsidies & Funds, Regional Funds, Pre-sales, Other) and **Contribution types**.
- **VAT rules**.

Rename, reorder, add or deactivate. A status that is in use cannot be deleted.

## Phases, activities, resources and links

The building blocks of templates: the phases of a production, the activities within them, the resources an activity needs, and the links between them. See [Templates, price lists and items](/help/budgeting/templates-price-lists-and-items/).

## Layouts, process flows and automations

- **Submenu**: which items the Budgeting and Production tabs of a project show, and in which order.
- **Process flows**: the steps of the budget editor.
- **Automations**: actions Tubes performs on an event, such as a status change.
- **Payment approval rules**: see [Payment approval rules](/help/approvals/payment-approval-rules/).

## Data lists and AI

Price lists, items, building blocks, logo lists and the assistants with their prompts. Each has its own page under Settings.

> **Tip:** Change lists before a production starts, not during. Renaming a status is harmless; deleting one that reports depend on is not.
