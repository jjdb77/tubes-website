## Connect

1. Open the workspace setup panel and choose **Slack**.
2. Tubes shows an app manifest. Create the app in your Slack workspace from that manifest, then authorise it with the button in Tubes.

## Channels per project

Once connected, a project can have its own Slack channel. Notifications about that production (a call sheet sent, a transaction waiting for approval) go to that channel; general notifications go to the channel you choose as default.

## Disconnect

Remove the app in Slack or disconnect it in Tubes; nothing else in the account changes.
