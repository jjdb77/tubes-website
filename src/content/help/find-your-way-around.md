The sidebar on the left is the main navigation. Which items you see depends on your role: an item you have no permission for is hidden automatically, and you can hide items you do not use yourself.

## Two layouts

Open **Preferences** at the bottom of the sidebar to choose a layout:

**Classic: Overview and Lists**

- *Overview*: Budgeting, Production, Pipeline, Budgets, Planning, Compare, Finance and Approvals. Stakeholder accounts also see Portfolio.
- *Lists*: Projects, Contacts, Cast & crew, Locations, Quotations and Chat.

**Concept: Budget, Plan, Produce**

- *Budget*: Budgeting, Pipeline, Budgets, Quotations, Compare and Contacts.
- *Plan*: Planning, Projects, Cast & crew and Locations.
- *Produce*: Production, Approvals, Finance, Chat and Portfolio.

Both layouts contain the same screens, grouped differently. The choice is personal and is remembered for you.

## What the main items are for

- **Budgeting** and **Production** are overviews of the projects in that phase. Click a project card to preview it.
- **Pipeline** lists projects and budgets that are not yet confirmed.
- **Budgets** lists every budget in the account.
- **Planning** is the calendar, timeline and crew view across all productions.
- **Compare** puts budgets side by side.
- **Finance** is the cost hub: review, approval, export and all transactions.
- **Approvals** shows what is waiting for you, with a badge for the number of pending items.
- **Projects**, **Contacts**, **Cast & crew** and **Locations** are the lists behind the productions.
- **Quotations** lists the documents you sent to clients.
- **Chat** is the team conversation per project, with a badge for unread messages.

## Bottom of the sidebar

- **Preferences**: layout, hidden items and your personal settings.
- **Account**: profile, password and security, accounts, connected accounts, billing and invoices.
- **Updates** and **What's New**: your notifications and the release notes for Tubes.
- **Help**: the Tubes assistant. Ask a question about Tubes and it answers in the context of the page you are on.
- **Feedback**: tell us that something is missing, unclear, or that you have a wish or idea.

> **Tip:** The blue coach marks that appear on some screens explain what to do next. Choose **Got it** to close one, or **Don't show again** once you know the screen.
