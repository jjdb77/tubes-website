Tubes sends quotations and other documents for digital signing through **DocuSign** or **DocuSeal**. Signed documents are returned to the project file.

## Connect

An administrator connects one of the two under **Integrations**:

- **DocuSign**: choose Connect and authorise Tubes in your DocuSign account. Tubes receives a message from DocuSign when a document is signed.
- **DocuSeal**: enter the connection details of your DocuSeal account.

Digital signing is an optional feature; ask Tubes if it is not on your plan.

## Sign a quotation

1. Open the quotation and switch **Signing** on. The button shows "Signing required" when it is on.
2. Add the signers: the client contact, and your own signer if the quotation is countersigned.
3. Choose **Send via DocuSign** (or the DocuSeal equivalent). The signers receive the document by email from the signing service.
4. When everyone has signed, the quotation status becomes **Signed** and the signed PDF is attached to the project.

## Budget and project signers

Budgets and projects can carry signers too, for internal sign-off. See [Budget approval and signers](/help/approvals/budget-approval-and-signers/).
