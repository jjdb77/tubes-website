The scene tools live in the project, under **Budgeting** (Scripts, Scenes) and **Production** (Scenes & Breakdown, Stripbook, Scene days).

## Scripts and scenes

Upload the script under **Scripts**. Create the scenes under **Scenes**: number, heading, location, day or night, and a short description. Each scene can carry the cast, the locations and the items it needs; this is the **breakdown**.

## Scene days

Link scenes to planned days. The call sheet of that day lists them in order, and the stripbook shows what is scheduled when.

## Stripbook

The stripbook is the classic strip view: one strip per scene, in shooting order, grouped by day. Drag strips to reorder. It is the quickest way to see whether a location is visited twice or a cast member is called on three separate days for four lines.

## Storyboards

Under the storyboard you sketch shots per scene: a shot list with type, movement and notes. Reorder shots, generate a shot list, and export the storyboard as PDF. Tubes can suggest shots for a scene and generate an image for a shot with AI; review the results as you would a draft. A storyboard can be shared by link, and the reader can leave comments.

> **Note:** AI images and suggestions use the AI credits of your account. See [Let AI draft a budget](/help/budgeting/let-ai-draft-a-budget/) for how credits work.
