## The Tubes assistant

The **Help** button in the sidebar opens the Tubes assistant. Type a question about Tubes, such as "How do I register invoices and costs?" and it answers with the page you are on as context. Empty screens also offer a ready-made question, for example "How do clients work in Tubes?"

An administrator can give the assistant extra context per page under **Settings > AI Help Assistant**.

## Coach marks

On screens that are new to you, short blue notes explain what to do next: "Time to build your budget", "Save a version of your budget", "Compare budgets side by side". Choose **Got it** to close a note, or **Don't show again** to hide it for good.

## What's New

**What's New** in the sidebar lists the updates and improvements to Tubes. New notifications about your own work (an accepted invitation, a transaction stuck without approvers) appear under **Updates**.

## Feedback

The **Feedback** button lets you report that something is missing, something is unclear, or that you have a wish or idea. Describe it and send; the Tubes team reads every message.

## Contact support

Cannot find an answer? Email [contact@tubes.media](mailto:contact@tubes.media). A screenshot of the screen and a note of which project it concerns help us answer quickly.
