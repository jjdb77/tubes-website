A Tubes account can have one of three roles: an ordinary production company, a vendor (VMS), or a **stakeholder**. A stakeholder account, for example a group, a financier or a parent company, does not run productions itself but follows the companies that do.

## What Portfolio shows

**Portfolio** in the sidebar lists every connected company and, per company, every production, with:

- **Sales**: the sales value of the budgets.
- **Invoiced**: what has been invoiced to clients.
- **Still expected**: what is still to be invoiced.
- **EFC**: the expected final cost.
- **Margin** and **Result**.

Switch between **Month**, **Quarter** and **Year**, and filter on one company or all companies.

## Cashflow across the portfolio

The cashflow tab rolls up money in from financing tranches and money out from dated committed costs, over every connected company. See [Financing, tranches and cashflow](/help/relations/financing-tranches-and-cashflow/) for how a single production builds that picture.

## Access

A production company connects a stakeholder from its own account; the stakeholder sees the figures, not the underlying invoices. Contact Tubes to set up a stakeholder account.
