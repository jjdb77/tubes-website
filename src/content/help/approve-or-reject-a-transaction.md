## Your queue

Open **Approvals** in the sidebar. The **Pending** tab shows every transaction that needs your approval, across all projects: supplier, reference, project, amount and the approvers with their level. The summary says how many are waiting across how many projects. When there is nothing, it says "Nothing to review".

## Check

Open a transaction. You see the document, the lines with their cost types and projects, the linked purchase orders as chips, and the issues the rules raised with their severity.

## Decide

- **Approve**: the transaction moves to the next level, or to finance when you were the last.
- **Reject**: it goes back to the controller with your note. The supplier is not informed automatically; use **Request correction** for that.
- **Waive**: approve despite a warning, with a note that says why. A block cannot be waived; it must be resolved.

Approved transactions move to the **History** tab, where you can look back at what you approved and when.

## Nobody can approve

If a transaction is waiting for a level that has no approver on the project, choose **Notify missing approvers**. The project owner receives a notification to assign someone under **Project team**.

> **Tip:** The badge on **Approvals** in the sidebar shows your pending count. Approvals are also in the mobile app as read-only; the decision itself is made on the web.
