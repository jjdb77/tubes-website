The planning screen has two modes, and each member of the team chooses their own.

## Simple

Simple keeps the calendar and the plan panel. You see the days, you open a day, you add people, locations and items. It is enough for most crew and for producers who want to see what is planned without the machinery.

## Advanced

Advanced adds:

- the **timeline** with the windows of the activities;
- the **budget bridge**: budget, planned and spent per header, always in view;
- **capacity** and **workload** views across productions;
- more lenses: board, crew swimlanes, costs and the breakdown;
- the **Show layers**.

## Show layers

In advanced mode, **Show** switches extra information on the calendar on and off:

- **Costs**: what each day costs from the budget lines.
- **Weather**: the forecast for shoot days with a location.
- **Status**: draft, published, to do, in progress, done.
- **Details**: people, locations and items on the day itself.
- **Windows**: the activity windows behind the days.

## Switching

Open the planning and switch the mode in the toolbar. Tubes remembers it for you; your colleagues keep their own choice.
