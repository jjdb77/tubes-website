**Locations** holds the places you shoot: locations, studios, offices, with address and coordinates so they show on the map.

## A location

Name, address, type, the **bureau** or owner it comes through, the **agreements** (what was agreed, for which dates), and the **reviews** your team left after a shoot. Availability types are internal, contract or open; statuses run from available to optioned, blocked, maintenance and cleanup.

## On a production

In the project, **Locations** under Budgeting or Production lists the locations of this production. A location on a planned day appears on the call sheet with its meet-up place and time, and the weather layer in the planning shows the forecast for it.

## Compare locations

Tick two or three locations in the list and choose **Compare locations** to see them side by side: attributes as rows, locations as columns. Remove a column or change the selection without starting over.

> **Tip:** Looking for new places? The free [locations guide on tubes.media](/compare-film-tv-locations/) has thousands of locations and studios across Europe, outside your Tubes account.
