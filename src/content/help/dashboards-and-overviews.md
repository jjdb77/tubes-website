## The dashboard

The dashboard is built from cards. **Pin** a card to keep it at the top, **collapse** it, drag to reorder, or remove it. Cards include:

- **Budget KPIs**: approved budgets, budgets in progress, quotations outstanding, and the conversion from concept to approved.
- **Budgets generated per week**.
- The financial overview and summary across productions: budgets, work budgets, actuals and expected margins per production, without manual consolidation.

## Overviews

The sidebar overviews show the productions by phase:

- **Projects in Budgeting**: productions being budgeted and quoted.
- **Projects in Production**: productions that are being planned and shot, with their forecast status.
- **Projects in Pipeline** and **Budgets in Pipeline**: what is not yet confirmed, with the amounts at stake.

Click a project card to preview it, or open it.

## Reports

The **Projects report** lists all productions with client, status, budget and result. Export it to Excel from the report.

## Portfolio

Stakeholder accounts (a group, a financier, a parent company) see **Portfolio**: the connected companies and productions side by side. See [Portfolio view for stakeholders](/help/reporting/portfolio-for-stakeholders/).
