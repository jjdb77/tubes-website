A call sheet always belongs to a project and is generated from a planned day. Link the activity to a project first if it has none.

## Generate

1. Open the day in the planning and choose **Call sheet** in the activity panel, or go to **Call Sheets** in the project and create one for the day.
2. Tubes fills in what the day already knows: date, start time, the people, the locations and the linked scenes.

## Complete the call sheet

A call sheet has sections you fill in or check:

- **Scenes**: the scenes of the day, in order.
- **Cast & crew**: everyone on the day, with call times and their role.
- **Specifications**: equipment and requirements per department, for example camera or sound.
- **Locations**: address, meet-up place and meet-up time, and the company move when the unit changes place during the day.
- **Notes**: the general notes, emergency information and anything the crew must know.

You can **duplicate** a call sheet for a similar day.

## Statuses

A call sheet is a **Draft** until you send it. It becomes **Sent**, then **Confirmed** when the crew has confirmed, and **Wrapped** after the day. A sent call sheet keeps the day as a call sheet; you cannot turn it back into a plain day.

## Send

Choose **Send call sheet**. Email goes out now: the call sheet as PDF, a calendar file, and each person's own confirmation link. WhatsApp and push are noted for later. See [Crew confirmations](/help/planning/crew-confirmations/).

## On the phone

Published call sheets appear in the Tubes mobile app under **Callsheets**, with cast & crew, locations, scenes, specifications and the production log, where crew can add a remark during the day. See [The Tubes mobile app](/help/planning/the-tubes-mobile-app/).
