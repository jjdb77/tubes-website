Clients are the companies and people you make productions for. Link them to projects, budgets and quotations, and keep contact details and invoice addresses in one place.

## Add a client

1. Open **Contacts** in the sidebar and choose **New client**.
2. Fill in the company name, the client type (broadcaster, agency, brand, co-producer), the status, and the invoice address and VAT number.
3. Add the contact persons: name, role, email and phone.

An administrator maintains the client types and statuses under **Settings**.

## Where a client appears

- On the **project**, as the client of the production.
- On the **budget** and the **quotation**, with the invoice address on the document.
- On **Involved companies** of a project when a client is one of several parties, for example a co-producer next to a broadcaster.

## Portal access

A contact can be given access to the client portal to see their projects and submit invoices. See [What the portal is](/help/portal/what-the-portal-is/).

## Suppliers

Suppliers are contacts too. When an invoice comes in, Tubes matches the supplier by name and VAT number, so a complete supplier record saves corrections later. With Xero connected, contacts can be synced both ways. See [Connect Xero](/help/imports-exports/connect-xero/).
