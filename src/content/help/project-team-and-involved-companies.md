## Project team

Under **Project team** you assign the Tubes users who work on this production and their role on it. This decides:

- who sees the project, when their account role does not give access to all projects;
- who approves the costs of this project, and at which level. See [How approval works](/help/approvals/how-approval-works/).

## Project roles and castings

**Project roles** are the functions on the production (director, DOP, producer). **Castings** link people from Cast & crew to those roles. The call sheet uses them.

## Involved companies

**Involved companies** lists every company with a part in the production besides the client: co-producers, a broadcaster, a fund, a service company. Each with its role. Financing entries are linked to these companies. See [Financing, tranches and cashflow](/help/relations/financing-tranches-and-cashflow/).

## Signers

Default signers per project decide who must sign off budgets and quotations of this production. See [Budget approval and signers](/help/approvals/budget-approval-and-signers/).
