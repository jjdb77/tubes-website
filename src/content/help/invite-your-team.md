## Invite

1. Open the workspace setup panel or your account menu and choose **Team**.
2. Choose **Invite User**, enter the email address and choose the role.
3. Send. The person receives "(you) invited you to (account)" with a link. If they already have a Tubes account they log in to accept; otherwise they create one, and the invitation is accepted for them.

You get a notification when an invitation is accepted.

## Owner, admin and member

Every account has one **owner**, who can transfer ownership to someone else. **Admins** manage settings, team and billing. **Members** work in the projects their role gives access to. On top of this, a **role** decides exactly which screens and actions someone may use. See [Roles and permissions](/help/settings/roles-and-permissions/).

## Access per project

A member whose role does not cover all projects only sees the projects they are on. Add them under **Project team** of the project.

## Remove someone

Remove a member from **Team**. Their planning, approvals and history stay in the account; they simply can no longer sign in to it.

> **Tip:** Crew who only need call sheets and their schedule do not need to be members. Send them call sheets with confirmation links, or invite them to the mobile app. See [The Tubes mobile app](/help/planning/the-tubes-mobile-app/).
