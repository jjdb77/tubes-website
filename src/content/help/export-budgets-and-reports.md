## Budgets

From the budget toolbar:

- **Print** produces the budget as a PDF, with the totals and the level of detail you choose. **Print production** is the version for the production team, without sales prices.
- **Export** writes the budget to Excel, with the categories, lines, quantities, rates and totals, so it can be read by a co-producer or fund that works in spreadsheets.
- The **viewer** shows the budget read-only in the browser, for a screen share or a link inside your team.

## Quotations

**Print** a quotation to PDF, or **Print with template** to apply another quotation template to the same content. See [Create a quotation](/help/budgeting/create-a-quotation/).

## Forecast and cost report

The forecast has **Print**, a print of the comparison of two snapshots, and a print of the tag view. The cost report prints as a document for financiers. See [The cost report](/help/reporting/the-cost-report/).

## Costs

Approved transactions are exported in batches to Excel or to your accounting system. See [Export approved costs](/help/imports-exports/export-approved-costs/).

## Storyboards

A storyboard exports as PDF, with or without the shot list.
