A budget holds the estimated costs of a production, organised in cost categories and budget lines. Every project can have several budgets: a first estimate, a client version, a revised version after the recce.

![Projects in budgeting with drafts and quotations](/assets/images/tubes-budgeting.png)

## Three ways to start

- **From a template**: your standard production structure with phases, activities and items. You adjust quantities and prices.
- **From an existing budget**: **Duplicate** creates an independent copy as the starting point for a new version.
- **With AI**: **Generate AI Budget** drafts line items, quantities and prices from your project description. You review and adjust. See [Let AI draft a budget](/help/budgeting/let-ai-draft-a-budget/).

You can also import a budget from Movie Magic Budgeting. See [Import a Movie Magic budget](/help/imports-exports/import-a-movie-magic-budget/).

## Budget and Sales

Two totals update instantly as you add or change lines:

- **Budget** is your cost total: what the production is expected to cost you.
- **Sales** is what you charge the client. Sales prices per line, additional costs, discounts and fees determine the difference, which shows as **Margin**.

## The budget toolbar

Each budget has its own set of screens:

- **Budget setup** and **Build budget**: the details and the editor. See [Build a budget](/help/budgeting/build-a-budget/).
- **Planning**: the activities of this budget, the days they run on and the crew booked for them.
- **Documents**: create and send quotes or cost confirmations to the client for review and approval before publication.
- **Compare**: this budget side by side with other budgets.
- **Analysis**: cost breakdown by phase and category, with variance reporting.
- **Overview & Export**: dashboard with totals, key metrics and export.
- **Publication**: publish the approved budget to make it available to the production team.

## Statuses

A budget moves through statuses such as Concept, Active, Approved, Completed and Cancelled. An administrator can rename these and add others under **Settings > Budget statuses**. Approving a budget can also create the work budget for production; see [Publish a budget and create a work budget](/help/budgeting/publish-a-budget-and-work-budget/).

> **Tip:** Keep one budget per purpose. A quotation, a published version and a work budget are all derived from a budget, so a clear name ("Client v2", "Post-recce") saves confusion later.
