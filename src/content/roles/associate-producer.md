The documentary is nearly locked, and twelve archive clips still have no signed licence. The associate producer spends a week on the phone with archives, agrees prices, updates the clearance list and chases the last signature on the day the cut goes to the broadcaster. The associate producer is the person who takes a defined part of the producer's work and sees it through.

## What an associate producer does

The title means different things on different projects. On a feature film it is often a credit for someone who helped the [producer](/production-roles/producer/) in a specific way, such as finding the story, bringing in a contact or handling post-production. On documentaries and factual television it is a real job with clear tasks. The associate producer researches, finds contributors, sets up shoots, handles archive and rights, and often directs or films smaller sequences.

They usually join in late development or pre-production and stay through delivery. What they deliver depends on the deal with the producer. It might be the archive clearances, the contributor releases, the delivery paperwork or the day-to-day link with the edit.

## Responsibilities

- Research stories, contributors, archive and locations
- Arrange shoots, travel and permissions
- Clear archive footage, music and other third-party material
- Keep the release forms and paperwork complete
- Act as the producer's link to the edit and the [post-production supervisor](/production-roles/post-production-supervisor/)
- Prepare delivery documents for the broadcaster or distributor

## How the role touches the budget

Usually within a clear limit. An associate producer may hold the budget line for archive, music or research travel and has to stay within it. They negotiate licence fees, raise purchase orders and pass invoices on for approval. They rarely see the whole budget, but the lines they own often move late in post, when clips are found and prices agreed. That makes their numbers important for the final [cost report](/insights/what-is-a-production-cost-report/).

## How people get into the role

Many associate producers start as a [researcher](/production-roles/researcher/) or [production coordinator](/production-roles/production-coordinator/) and take on more responsibility over a few projects. In factual television it is a common step between researcher and producer or director. On features the credit is sometimes given to an assistant to the producer, a development executive or someone who brought something essential to the project.

## In TV and documentary

In factual television the associate producer is a working title with daily tasks, often under a [series producer](/production-roles/series-producer/). In drama and features it is more often a credit than a job description, so it is worth asking what the person actually did.
