A new comedy is ready, but where should it go? Thursday at nine follows a strong drama, but the audience there is older. Friday at ten is younger, but up against a big entertainment show on another channel. The scheduler compares the options, checks the rights and the compliance notes, and proposes Thursday at ten with a repeat on Sunday. The scheduler is the person who decides when each programme reaches the audience.

## What a scheduler does

A linear TV channel broadcasts around the clock, and every slot needs a programme. The scheduler builds that grid, usually months ahead. They place new commissions from the [commissioning editors](/production-roles/commissioning-editor/), bought-in films and series from the [head of acquisitions](/production-roles/head-of-acquisitions/), and repeats from the archive. The big strategic choices, such as which night gets the flagship drama, are made with the [channel controller](/production-roles/channel-controller/).

Placing a programme involves more than taste. The scheduler checks whether the channel still has the right to show a title, how many runs are left on the licence, whether it is suitable for the time of day according to the [compliance manager](/production-roles/compliance-manager/), and what competing channels are showing. After transmission they study the audience figures and adjust. The final schedule goes to the transmission team, where the [playout operator](/production-roles/playout-operator/) puts it on air.

## Responsibilities

- Build the daily, weekly and seasonal programme grid
- Place commissions, acquisitions and repeats in suitable slots
- Check rights, licence runs and time-slot suitability
- Plan around events such as sport, holidays and elections
- Study audience figures and adjust the schedule
- Advise commissioners on which slots need new programmes
- Prepare schedule information for the TV guide and listings

## How the role touches the budget

Through the cost of each hour on air. A channel's programme budget has to fill every slot, and the scheduler helps decide where expensive originals go and where cheaper repeats and acquisitions fill the rest. Using a repeat run that has already been paid for saves money, and an unused run may be wasted when the licence expires. The scheduler does not manage production budgets, but their plan of empty slots tells commissioners what to buy and at roughly what price.

## How people become schedulers

Most start as a scheduling assistant, logging programmes, durations and rights in the scheduling system. A good knowledge of audiences and ratings, a feeling for timing and a careful eye for detail are needed. Some move on to channel strategy, acquisitions or a controller role.

## At a streamer

Streamers have no linear grid, but the work still exists. Someone decides which titles launch on which day, which ones appear on the home screen, and when a title leaves the service because its licence ends.
