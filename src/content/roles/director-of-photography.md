The director wants the kitchen scene to feel like the walls are closing in. The director of photography suggests a longer lens, a lower ceiling light and a camera that slowly drifts closer over two minutes. Nobody in the audience will notice any of it, but everyone will feel it. The director of photography, or DoP, is the person who turns the director's ideas into images.

## What a director of photography does

The DoP works for the [director](/production-roles/director/) on the creative side and for the [producer](/production-roles/producer/) on what it may cost. They join in [pre-production](/how-a-film-is-made/#pre-production), often weeks before the shoot. Together with the director they decide the look of the film. That covers the camera and lenses, the aspect ratio (the shape of the frame), the colour and the style of the light, and how much the camera moves. They visit locations, test cameras and make a shot list for difficult scenes.

On set the DoP leads three departments. The camera crew works the camera, the [gaffer](/production-roles/gaffer/) and the electricians build the light, and the [key grip](/production-roles/key-grip/) and the grips handle everything the camera stands on or moves along. After the shoot the DoP usually returns for the grade, the session where the [colourist](/production-roles/colourist/) sets the final colour of every shot.

## Responsibilities

- Agree the visual style of the film with the director
- Choose camera, lenses, filters and lighting approach, and run camera tests
- Scout locations with an eye on light, space and power
- Plan the coverage of each scene (which shots are needed to tell it)
- Lead the camera, lighting and grip departments on set
- Decide how each shot is lit and framed, often operating the camera on smaller productions
- Check the images daily and supervise the colour grade in [post-production](/how-a-film-is-made/#post-production)

## How the role touches the budget

The DoP does not manage money directly, but many of their choices cost money. The camera package, the lighting truck, cranes, extra crew on big days and the number of hours needed to light a scene all come from their requests. The [line producer](/production-roles/line-producer/) agrees an equipment list and crew size with the DoP in pre-production. During the shoot, slow lighting set-ups show up as overtime, so a DoP who works fast saves real money.

## How people become directors of photography

Many DoPs start in the camera department as trainee or [second assistant camera](/production-roles/second-assistant-camera/), then move up to focus puller and [camera operator](/production-roles/camera-operator/). Others come from film school and shoot short films, music videos and commercials until they get a feature or a series. A showreel of their work matters more than a diploma.

## In TV and commercials

In the US the title is often "director of photography" or "DP", in Europe "DoP" or "cinematographer". On TV series several DoPs may share the episodes, each following a look set in the first block. In commercials the DoP works on short, intense shoots and the client and agency often approve the look in advance with reference images.
