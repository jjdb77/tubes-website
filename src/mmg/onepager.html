For Martin Waterman and the team at Motion Media Group. Tubes is production software with management overviews on top, for one or more underlying production companies: the companies run their productions in Tubes Production, and MMG follows every production, every company and all companies together in Tubes Portfolio. Same live data, no manual consolidation in between. This onepager is the short version; the full proposal alongside it explains how each part works.

Financial benefits

  1. Overruns surface at commitment time, weeks before they reach a cost report, while rescoping a shoot day or renegotiating a vendor is still possible, instead of a write-off.
  2. Group consolidation stops being manual. The portfolio view is built from the teams' own live data, finance hours go back to finance, and re-keying errors go away.
  3. Cashflow planned on numbers, not estimates. Forecasts per production company by month, quarter and year show when capital goes out and comes back, the timing a capital-recycling model runs on, with margin forecast alongside.
  4. Investor reporting from live numbers. Read-only links replace assembled quarterly spreadsheets, faster reporting cycles, and a transparency story that sells.
  5. No switching cost. Movie Magic budgets import directly; teams change nothing on day one and adopt more when it pays.
  6. No implementation cost. Self-onboarding via a signup link in 5–10 minutes, no consultants, no IT project, built to start fast.
  7. One system instead of five. Budgeting, cost control, invoices, planning, call sheets and crew communication on one data set, fewer tools to license, sync and reconcile across jurisdictions.

In short

  • Two versions: MMG follows in Tubes Portfolio; companies run in Tubes Production.
  • Every production connected: budget → PO → invoice → forecast, live.
  • Early warnings and AI analysis at group level, drill-down to the invoice.
  • Cashflow and margin forecast per company, month, quarter, year.
  • Movie Magic imports directly, adoption is gradual, no forced migration.
  • Self-onboarding via your signup link, 5–10 minutes, standard setup and optional templates applied.
  • International by default: each company in its local currency, with fixed exchange rates per production; the 10 major Western languages, per company or user.
  • Price per minute and per episode, unit costs comparable across productions.
  • Investor-ready: read-only share links on live numbers.
  • Support included: help at signup, training when needed, hands-on help to get companies going.
  • Proposal: a pilot with one of the productions you finance.
